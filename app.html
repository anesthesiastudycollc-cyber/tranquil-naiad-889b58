What You Work Through

A case, from the pre-op page to the debrief

The workbook follows the shape of a real anesthetic, prompting for the decisions at the point they are actually made.

01

Preoperative review

Comorbidities, airway assessment, medications held and continued, fasting status, and the risk factors that change the plan — captured before anything else is decided.

02

Induction

An agent decision tree that asks what the patient in front of you can tolerate, with dosing prompts and the reasoning written next to the choice rather than after it.

03

Maintenance

Depth, ventilation, fluids, and hemodynamic targets tracked as a checklist you can work down mid-case, with space for what you would change and why.

04

Emergence

Reversal, extubation criteria, and the handover summary — the part of a plan most often left until it is already happening.

05

Crisis response

Structured walk-throughs for the events worth rehearsing before you meet them, so the sequence is familiar rather than improvised.
