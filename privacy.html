Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-07-25

This Privacy Policy explains how Anesthesia Study Co. LLC handles information through its website, educational products, and interactive anesthesia planning tools.

Support URL

Support page: https://anesthesiastudyco.com/#support
Support email: admin@anesthesiastudyco.com

Educational Purpose

The website, app, and products are for educational purposes only and are not medical advice. They are intended to support learning, study, simulation preparation, and clinical reasoning practice.

Information We Collect

  • This website does not collect personal information through general browsing forms.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address and message content so we can respond.
  • If you buy a digital product on this website, our payment processor collects the payment and contact details you enter, and we receive your email address, the amount paid, and the list of items purchased so we can deliver your files and provide support.
  • Purchases made through Etsy, Shopify, Amazon, or other third-party platforms are handled by those platforms.

Purchases and Digital Delivery

Card payments on this website are processed by Stripe, Inc. Your full card details are entered on Stripe's own checkout page and are never received or stored by Anesthesia Study Co. LLC. Stripe's handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy at https://stripe.com/privacy.

After a successful payment we store a record of the order — the payment reference, the email address given at checkout, the total paid, and the items purchased — so that we can confirm entitlement to a download, answer support requests, and meet business record-keeping obligations. We do not sell or share this information for marketing purposes.

Purchased files are delivered through private download links that expire 30 days after purchase. Anyone holding a link can use it until it expires, so please do not share your download links.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to support or business inquiries.
  • To deliver purchased digital products and confirm access to downloads.
  • To provide customer support for educational products.
  • To improve clarity, usability, and educational value of our resources.

Third-Party Links

This website may link to Stripe, Etsy, Shopify, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest, Amazon, or other platforms. Those platforms have their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies and Tracking

This website does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts by default. Hosting providers may collect standard technical logs required to operate the website.

Children's Privacy

Our products are intended for adult learners, anesthesia students, educators, and clinicians. They are not directed toward children.

Data Security

No internet transmission is completely secure. We use reasonable small-business measures to protect communications and respond to support requests.
